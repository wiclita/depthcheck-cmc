Explore the sources, assumptions, and limitations +

The 0.1% and 1% bands are illustrative heuristics, not validated safety thresholds. A small footprint does not mean a trade is safe. Turnover = reported 24h volume ÷ market cap × 100. Volume changes compare 24-hour periods. Columns are calculated automatically, not manually assigned.

Live mode uses GET /v1/cryptocurrency/listings/latest?limit=100&convert=USD: the first 100 assets in CMC's default listings order. Responses are cached server-side for 60 seconds; refreshing inside that window does not request new CMC data. There is no automatic polling.

Reported volume may include activity that does not reflect actual liquidity. This screen does not account for spread, exchange-specific depth, fees, fragmentation, or execution probability. Missing data stays unknown, never zero.

Demo mode uses synthetic illustrative data, not current prices. Quotes for real assets are also synthetic; SMALL, THIN, and NODATA are fictional example assets.

The fetched time is server retrieval time, not the market quote time. Each asset's CMC update timestamp is preserved in the response below. Price, volume and turnover are denominated in USD where applicable. A meter is capped at 1% for display only; the printed footprint is not capped.
